# Catalogue reference image credits

All catalogue records generated by `seed_demo_products` are demo listings. Reference photos identify a brand or accessory type only; they do not assert that the exact pictured model is available at Venky Mobiles. Prices, specifications, colours, and inventory remain unverified.

Images are stored under `backend/media/products/demo/` and attached to individual `ProductImage` rows by product slug. The homepage uses the same Apple reference photo at `public/catalog/apple-iphone-reference.jpg`. Admin-uploaded product images take precedence and are preserved by `attach_demo_product_images`.

The reference photos are reproduced locally for a stable catalogue experience. The listed attribution and license links remain available here. No image was taken from Amazon or Flipkart.

The homepage hero currently uses `iphone-18-pro-max-premium.jpg`, a locally stored copy of the owner-supplied image reference link. Confirm usage rights with the shop owner before publishing this asset publicly; replace it from the same hero slot if a licensed studio photo is provided.

The charger, screen protector, and over-ear headphones demo records use their category fallbacks until a locally stored reference photo is available. They do not reuse a smartphone photograph.
